# IRC:SP:55-2014 — Guidelines on Safety in Road Construction Zones (Second Revision)
## Structured Extraction for AURIS DPR Analyzer Pro

> **Source:** IRC:SP:55-2014, Indian Roads Congress, Second Revision
> **Scope:** Mandatory safety requirements for road construction, maintenance and utility work zones on all categories of roads (NH, SH, MDR, ODR, urban and rural).
> **Excludes:** Emergency works of duration <60 minutes; permanent road safety design (covered by IRC:SP:88 — Road Safety Audit).
> **Format:** Mandatory requirements, design tables, formulas and DPR compliance checklist. Theory and worked examples excluded.

> **Note on labelling:** This document is officially "Guidelines on Safety in Road Construction Zones". Road Safety Audit of the *permanent* design is covered by **IRC:SP:88-2019** (separate code). DPR findings about RSA of the permanent design should cite IRC:SP:88, not IRC:SP:55. DPR findings about construction-phase safety (TTC plan, work zone layout, signage during construction, traffic diversion) should cite IRC:SP:55 (this document).

> **Working extraction:** Structured for DPR audit use; cross-check clause numbering against the published PDF before quoting verbatim in regulatory submissions.

## PART 1: SCOPE, APPLICABILITY & DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Mandatory Application (Cl. 1.3)
This code applies to **all** road construction, reconstruction, widening, strengthening, utility installation and maintenance works irrespective of:
- Road category (Expressway, NH, SH, MDR, ODR, Village Road, Urban Road)
- Project duration (long-term, intermediate, short-term, mobile)
- Funding agency (Central, State, PPP, BRO, NHAI, MoRTH)

Emergency works of duration **less than 60 minutes** are excluded.

### 1.2 Mandatory Compliance (Cl. 1.4)
- A **Traffic Management Plan (TMP)** shall be prepared and approved by the Engineer-in-Charge **before** any work commences in the road right-of-way.
- The TMP shall form part of the DPR / contract documents.
- All construction zone temporary traffic control (TTC) devices shall conform to this code; non-conforming devices shall not be deployed.

### 1.3 Definitions (Cl. 2)
- **Work Zone:** Area between the first advance warning sign and the point where traffic returns to normal flow downstream of the work.
- **Activity Area:** Portion of the work zone where actual construction or maintenance occurs.
- **Advance Warning Area:** Section upstream of the work zone that informs approaching road users.
- **Transition Area:** Section where traffic is moved out of its normal path.
- **Buffer Area:** Longitudinal/lateral space between transition and activity area to absorb vehicle intrusion (no equipment, workers or materials permitted).
- **Termination Area:** Downstream section where traffic returns to normal flow.
- **TTC:** Temporary Traffic Control.
- **TMP:** Traffic Management Plan.

## PART 2: WORK ZONE CLASSIFICATION (MANDATORY)

### 2.1 Duration-Based Classification (Cl. 3.1)

**Table 3.1 — Work Zone Duration Categories**

| Type | Duration | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Long-Term Stationary | > 3 days at a single location | Major widening, embankment, bridge construction, pavement reconstruction |
| Intermediate-Term Stationary | Overnight to 3 days | Resurfacing, deck repair, utility installation |
| Short-Term Stationary | Within a single daylight period (< 12 h) | Pothole repair, signage installation, joint sealing |
| Short Duration | < 60 min at a location | Pavement marking, sign repair, minor patching |
| Mobile | Moving operation, no fixed location | Crack sealing, sweeping, line painting |

### 2.2 Mandatory Selection of TTC Devices (Cl. 3.2)
TTC device count and type shall be selected according to:
1. Work zone duration class (Table 3.1)
2. Road class and design speed
3. Carriageway configuration (single lane, two lane, divided)
4. Time of work (daylight, night, all weather)

Long-term stationary zones on NH/SH **shall** use Type-III barricades, retro-reflective signs and mandatory flashing warning lights.

## PART 3: WORK ZONE COMPONENTS (MANDATORY LAYOUT)

### 3.1 Five Mandatory Zone Components (Cl. 4.1)
Every work zone shall have the following five components in sequence in the direction of traffic flow:

1. **Advance Warning Area (A)** — alerts approaching traffic
2. **Transition Area (T)** — diverts traffic out of its normal path
3. **Buffer Area (B)** — longitudinal safety gap (vehicle intrusion absorption)
4. **Activity Area (W)** — where work actually occurs
5. **Termination Area (D)** — returns traffic to normal flow

### 3.2 Advance Warning Area — Minimum Length (Cl. 4.2.1)

**Table 4.1 — Minimum Advance Warning Area Length (A) by Design Speed**

| Design Speed (km/h) | Urban Roads (m) | Rural / NH–SH (m) | Expressway (m) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ 50 | 60 | 150 | — |
| 50 – 65 | 100 | 250 | — |
| 65 – 80 | 150 | 350 | 500 |
| 80 – 100 | — | 500 | 800 |
| > 100 | — | — | 1000 |

- For two-lane two-way roads, advance warning to be provided in **both directions**.
- Minimum **three** advance warning signs spaced at A/3 intervals.

### 3.3 Transition Area — Taper Length Formula (Cl. 4.3.1)

**Mandatory taper length (L) for lane closures:**

$$L = \frac{W \times V}{1.6} \quad \text{for } V \geq 60 \text{ km/h}$$

$$L = \frac{W \times V^2}{155} \quad \text{for } V < 60 \text{ km/h}$$

Where:
- L = taper length (m)
- W = lateral shift / width of closed lane (m)
- V = design speed of the road (km/h)

**Table 4.2 — Minimum Taper Length (L) for Standard 3.5 m Lane Closure**

| Design Speed V (km/h) | Formula Used | Minimum L (m) |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | W·V²/155 | 21 |
| 40 | W·V²/155 | 36 |
| 50 | W·V²/155 | 57 |
| 60 | W·V / 1.6 | 132 |
| 80 | W·V / 1.6 | 175 |
| 100 | W·V / 1.6 | 220 |
| 120 | W·V / 1.6 | 265 |

### 3.4 Buffer Area — Mandatory Lengths (Cl. 4.4)
- **Longitudinal buffer (between transition and activity):** Stopping Sight Distance (SSD) for the design speed; absolute minimum 30 m, maximum 150 m.
- **Lateral buffer (between live lane and activity):** Minimum 1.2 m for V ≤ 50 km/h; minimum 2.4 m for V > 50 km/h.
- No workers, equipment, vehicles or materials shall be placed in the buffer area.

### 3.5 Termination Area — Minimum Length (Cl. 4.5)
Length D ≥ 30 m for all configurations; a downstream taper at the same rate as the transition taper shall be provided where traffic resumes a different alignment.

## PART 4: TRAFFIC CONTROL DEVICES (MANDATORY)

### 4.1 Signs (Cl. 5.1)

**Table 5.1 — Mandatory Construction-Zone Sign Categories**

| Category | Background | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warning | Yellow (high-intensity retro-reflective Type IV) | Alert approaching traffic | "Men at Work", "Road Closed Ahead", "Lane Closed" |
| Regulatory | White with red border | Mandate action | "Stop", "Speed Limit 40", "Detour" |
| Guidance | Yellow with black text/arrow | Direct movement | "Diversion →", "Keep Left" |

- All construction-zone signs shall be **retro-reflective Type IV (high-intensity prismatic)** sheeting.
- Minimum sign size: 600 × 600 mm (urban); 900 × 900 mm (NH/SH).
- Signs shall be mounted at **1.5 m above road surface (rural)** or **2.0 m above pedestrian path (urban)**.
- Damaged, faded or non-retro-reflective signs **shall be replaced before next shift**.

### 4.2 Channelizing Devices (Cl. 5.2)

**Table 5.2 — Channelizing Device Spacing**

| Device | Application | Maximum Spacing — Taper | Maximum Spacing — Tangent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cones (≥ 450 mm height) | Short-term, daylight only | V/3 metres | V/2 metres |
| Drums (≥ 900 mm height) | All conditions | V/3 metres | V/2 metres |
| Tubular markers | Short-term lateral | V/3 metres | V/2 metres |
| Plastic barriers (water-filled) | Long-term, severity protection | Continuous | Continuous |

Where V = design speed in km/h, spacing in metres.

- All channelizing devices shall carry retro-reflective bands.
- Devices shall be weighted to resist wind from passing traffic.

### 4.3 Barricades (Cl. 5.3)

**Table 5.3 — Barricade Classification**

| Type | Description | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Type I | Single horizontal rail with retro-reflective sheeting | Short-term, low speed (< 50 km/h) |
| Type II | Two horizontal rails | Intermediate-term or moderate speed |
| Type III | Three or more horizontal rails, full-width (≥ 1500 mm) | Long-term, high speed (> 60 km/h), road closure |

- All barricade rails shall be 200 mm wide minimum.
- Alternate orange and white retro-reflective stripes at 100 mm pitch, slope **downward toward the side traffic is to pass**.

### 4.4 Lighting Devices (Cl. 5.4)
- Steady-burn lights mandatory on barricades at **night** in long-term zones.
- Flashing warning lights mandatory at the **first advance warning sign** of every work zone operating at night.
- Convoy / pace vehicle to carry roof-mounted amber rotating beacon, visible at 300 m minimum.

### 4.5 Pavement Markings (Cl. 5.5)
- Temporary markings shall be **yellow** (distinct from permanent white edge / lane lines).
- Conflicting permanent markings shall be **obliterated** (not merely overpainted) — by grinding, blasting or removable black tape.
- Raised pavement markers permitted; reflectorisation mandatory at night work.

### 4.6 Flagging (Cl. 5.6)
- Flagger shall wear high-visibility class-3 garment (jacket + trousers) with retro-reflective bands.
- Flagger shall be positioned in a **safe location with escape route**; distance from work area equal to or greater than SSD for the design speed.
- Flagger shall be **trained and certified** by the Engineer-in-Charge.
- Hand signal: STOP/SLOW paddle (450 × 450 mm) with retro-reflective faces.

## PART 5: SPEED CONTROL THROUGH WORK ZONES (MANDATORY)

### 5.1 Mandatory Speed Reduction (Cl. 6.1)

**Table 6.1 — Maximum Permissible Speed Through Work Zone**

| Road Design Speed (km/h) | Maximum Work Zone Speed (km/h) | Speed Drop |
|---|---|---|
| 120 | 80 | 33% |
| 100 | 65 | 35% |
| 80 | 50 | 38% |
| 65 | 40 | 38% |
| 50 | 30 | 40% |
| ≤ 40 | 20 | 50% |

- Speed reduction shall be achieved in **steps of not more than 20 km/h per warning sign**.
- The work zone speed limit shall be regulatory (mandatory) and enforceable.

### 5.2 Speed Reduction Devices (Cl. 6.2)
Permitted devices to enforce speed reduction:
- Successive speed-limit signs (mandatory)
- Rumble strips (transverse, three sets at 30 m, 60 m, 90 m upstream of activity area)
- Variable Message Signs (VMS) for long-term zones on NH/Expressway
- Speed display radar signs (recommended where compliance is poor)

**Speed humps are prohibited** in work zones on speeds > 50 km/h.

## PART 6: TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT PLAN — MANDATORY CONTENT (Cl. 7)

### 6.1 Mandatory TMP Components (Cl. 7.1)
Every TMP shall contain, at minimum:

1. **Site assessment** — existing road geometry, traffic volume, composition, design speed, sight distance restrictions.
2. **Work-zone classification** per Table 3.1.
3. **Risk assessment** — identified hazards and mitigation measures.
4. **Work-zone layout drawings** — with all five components (A, T, B, W, D) drawn to scale, showing all signs, channelizers, barricades and lighting.
5. **Sign schedule** — type, size, retro-reflective grade, mounting height, chainage of each sign.
6. **Traffic diversion plan** (if applicable) — alternate route, diversion length, capacity check, additional signage along diversion.
7. **Pedestrian / cyclist management** — separated walkway with overhead protection where pedestrian volumes > 100/h, cycle track diversion.
8. **Emergency response plan** — emergency vehicle access route, contact list, first-aid station location, fire equipment.
9. **Worker safety plan** — PPE, training, induction, drinking water, toilet provision.
10. **Communication plan** — public notification, hoardings, social-media advisories, helpline.
11. **Periodic safety audit schedule** — minimum frequency per Cl. 8.4.

### 6.2 TMP Approval (Cl. 7.2)
- TMP shall be prepared by a qualified safety engineer.
- TMP shall be approved by Engineer-in-Charge **before** the contractor mobilises.
- Any modification to the approved TMP requires re-approval.

### 6.3 Mandatory TMP Inclusion in DPR (Cl. 7.3)
- The DPR shall include a TMP appendix with all components above.
- A **construction-zone safety budget** shall be itemised in the BoQ (typical range 0.5–1.5% of construction cost; minimum 0.3%).
- A **safety officer** shall be designated in the project organisation chart.

## PART 7: SPECIAL CONDITIONS (MANDATORY)

### 7.1 Night Work (Cl. 8.1)
- All signs upgraded to **Type IV retro-reflective** (already mandatory under Cl. 5.1 — confirmed here).
- All channelizers shall carry steady-burn lights.
- Illumination on activity area: minimum **20 lux**, glare-controlled (no direct glare toward live traffic).
- High-visibility class-3 PPE mandatory for **all** personnel on site, including supervisors.

### 7.2 Adverse Weather (Cl. 8.2)
- Work shall **cease** when visibility falls below **the SSD for the design speed**.
- Additional advance warning signs and flashing lights required during fog/rain operations.
- Pavement-marking and bituminous works **shall not** be undertaken in rain.

### 7.3 Hill Roads (Cl. 8.3 — cross-reference IRC:SP:48)
- Single-lane controlled traffic with pilot vehicle escort permitted on hill sections; convoy method per IRC:SP:48 Cl. 7.3.
- Advance warning at **every hairpin bend** within 1 km of the work zone.
- Catch-water-drain blockage from construction debris **shall be cleared daily**.

### 7.4 Pedestrian Safety (Cl. 8.4)
- Continuous pedestrian path of **minimum 1.2 m width** shall be maintained around the work zone.
- Where the path crosses the activity area, **overhead protection (covered walkway)** is mandatory.
- Pedestrian guidance signs at every diversion.

### 7.5 Cyclist Safety (Cl. 8.5)
- Cycle tracks shall be diverted onto a parallel route or onto the carriageway with a **lateral buffer ≥ 1.5 m**.
- Markings of cycle diversion shall be in **green retro-reflective paint**.

## PART 8: WORKER SAFETY (MANDATORY)

### 8.1 Personal Protective Equipment (Cl. 9.1)
Mandatory PPE for **every** worker on site:
- Hard hat (BIS-certified)
- High-visibility class-2 vest (class-3 jacket+trousers for night work)
- Safety footwear with steel toecap
- Eye protection during cutting, grinding, drilling, hot-mix work
- Hearing protection in zones > 85 dB(A)
- Respirator during dust-generating works

### 8.2 Training (Cl. 9.2)
- Site induction within 24 h of joining; refresher every 6 months.
- Flagger / signaller certification by Engineer-in-Charge.
- Toolbox talk (≤ 15 min) before every shift.

### 8.3 Welfare Facilities (Cl. 9.3)
- Drinking water: minimum **5 litres per worker per day**.
- Toilet provision: 1 per 25 workers (segregated by gender).
- First-aid station with trained first-aider mandatory for sites > 50 workers.
- Shaded rest area with seating.

### 8.4 Worker–Traffic Separation (Cl. 9.4)
- Where work is within 3 m of live traffic at V > 50 km/h, a **physical barrier (Type-III barricade or water-filled barrier)** is mandatory.
- A safety officer dedicated to monitoring worker–traffic separation is mandatory for long-term zones on NH/SH.

## PART 9: WORK ZONE SAFETY AUDIT & INSPECTION (MANDATORY)

### 9.1 Periodic Inspection (Cl. 10.1)

**Table 10.1 — Minimum Inspection Frequency**

| Work Zone Type | Minimum Frequency |
|---|---|
| Long-Term Stationary | Daily (at shift start), full audit weekly |
| Intermediate-Term Stationary | Daily |
| Short-Term Stationary | At setup and at takedown |
| Short Duration / Mobile | Continuous (by site supervisor) |

### 9.2 Audit Checklist (Cl. 10.2)
Each periodic audit shall verify:
- [ ] All five zone components present and dimensioned per TMP
- [ ] All signs in place, legible, retro-reflective and at correct mounting height
- [ ] Channelizer spacing complies with Table 5.2
- [ ] Barricades undamaged, retro-reflective sheeting intact
- [ ] Buffer area clear of workers, equipment and materials
- [ ] PPE compliance by every worker
- [ ] Speed-control devices in place and operational
- [ ] Pedestrian / cyclist provisions maintained
- [ ] Lighting (night work) — operational and at minimum 20 lux
- [ ] Flagger present, trained, in safe location

### 9.3 Non-Compliance Action (Cl. 10.3)
- **Critical non-compliance** (missing barricade in live traffic, untrained flagger, no PPE, speed limit not enforced) — **work shall cease immediately** until corrected.
- **Major non-compliance** (faded sign, missing channelizer, buffer encroachment) — corrected within 24 h.
- **Minor non-compliance** — corrected within 7 days.

### 9.4 Audit Records (Cl. 10.4)
- Each audit recorded in a bound register with: date, time, auditor name, findings, corrective actions, follow-up due date.
- Records to be available for inspection by the Engineer-in-Charge at all times.
